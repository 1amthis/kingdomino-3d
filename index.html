How to play

Build the richest kingdom

Everyone starts with a castle. Each round you claim one domino and add it to your kingdom. When the dominoes run out, the kingdom with the most points wins.

Claim a domino with your king

New dominoes are laid out in number order. Put your king on the one you want. A low number lets you choose sooner next round; high numbers usually carry more crowns.

Connect it

A new domino must touch your castle, or touch a square of the same terrain. One matching side is enough.

Stay within 5 × 5

Your kingdom must fit in a 5×5 square (7×7 in the Mighty Duel); the glowing frame shows the room left. A domino that fits nowhere is discarded.

Crowns multiply

A property is a group of touching squares of one terrain. It scores squares × crowns, so without a crown it scores nothing. Optional rules add +10 for a centred castle and +5 for a full kingdom with no discard.
