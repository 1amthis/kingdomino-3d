How to play

Each player starts with a castle. Every round, dominoes are drawn from the chest, sorted by number and revealed. You claim one by placing your king on it — higher numbers usually have more crowns, but lower numbers let you choose first next round.

Placing a domino

  • It must touch your castle, or match the terrain of at least one neighbouring square.
  • Your whole kingdom must fit in a 5×5 grid (7×7 in the Mighty Duel) — the glowing frame shows the space left.
  • If a domino cannot be placed anywhere, it is discarded.

Scoring

Each property (connected squares of the same terrain) scores squares × crowns. A property with no crown scores nothing. Optional rules add +10 for a centred castle and +5 for a full kingdom with no discarded domino.

Playing online

Set a seat to Online friend, press Invite your friends and send the link. Each friend who opens it takes a free seat; press Start game once everyone has joined.
